# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Examples of
positive behavior include constructive technical disagreement, clear sourcing,
respect for privacy, and accepting corrections. Unacceptable behavior includes
harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publishing case data without
authorization, or deliberately submitting unsafe forensic guidance.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other public spaces where a person represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using a contact
method on their GitHub profiles. Do not include sensitive case material in a
public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when
necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
person who raises a good-faith concern is not tolerated.
